AI-Based Income Adaptive Traffic Fine System

The AI Based Income Adaptive Fine System is a smart system designed to calculate fines based on a person's income level instead of using a fixed fine amount. Traditional fine systems charge the same penalty to everyone, which may be unfair because people have different financial capacities. This system uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) to analyze a person's income and determine a fair and adaptive fine amount. The goal is to make penalties more equitable, balanced, and socially fair.
